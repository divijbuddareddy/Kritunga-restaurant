Our Heritage

The Rayalaseema Legacy

At Kritunga, every recipe is a testament to the fiery soil of Rayalaseema. We honour the timeless tradition of cooking in seasoned earthenware clay pots that seal in the rich juices of country chicken, tender mutton, and cold-pressed spices.

"We don't tone down our spices; we celebrate the uncompromising, smoky, and earthy soul of true Andhra gastronomy."
