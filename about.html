Roots of Rayalaseema

The Soul of Andhra Gastronomy

Born from the rugged, spice-blessed soil of Rayalaseema, Kritunga Restaurant was founded on a singular passion: to preserve and celebrate India’s most honest, fiery, and deeply comforting culinary heritage.

1. Seasoned Clay Pots

Our culinary hallmark is the unglazed, wood-fired earthenware pot. Clay breathes during the cooking process, circulating heat gently to seal in the juices of meats while imparting an irreplaceable rustic aroma.

2. Stone-Ground Spices

We source our red chillies directly from the sun-drenched fields of Guntur and dry roast them alongside whole coriander, cinnamon, and black pepper, grinding them with stone mills daily.

3. Desi Ghee & Fresh Herbs

From tangy Gongura leaves plucked fresh to velvety, aromatic country buffalo ghee, we refuse shortcuts. Every biryani and curry represents the uncompromising generosity of South Indian feasting.

Timeless Comfort

Ragi Mudde & Natukodi Pulao

In the heart of Rayalaseema, no feast is complete without wholesome finger-millet Ragi Mudde or authentic Natukodi (country chicken) cooked in an earthen pot. This nutritious, hearty food sustained generations of farmers and royal dynasties alike.

At our Marathahalli kitchen, our master chefs honour these ancient recipes with exact adherence to temperature, timing, and balance of spices.
